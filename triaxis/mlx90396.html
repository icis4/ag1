MLX90396

Triaxis three-axis magnetometer
The protocol here is the MLX90393/MLX90395 family protocol. Melexis publishes datasheets for the 90391 to 90395 and the 90397, but not for the 90396, so the command set, the 0x80 command register and the status byte are taken from its documented siblings and have not been confirmed against this part. The readings are raw LSB counts: converting them to microtesla needs the gain and resolution settings, which differ per variant. Treat a working readout as evidence the family protocol applies, and the register panel below as the way to check anything else.
