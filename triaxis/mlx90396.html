Triaxis three-axis magnetometer
Built from MLX90396 preliminary datasheet V0.5 (8 June 2026). Preliminary means the register map and the command encoding can still move. Two things the page cannot know about your part: whether CRC-8 protection is enabled in its NVRAM (the toggle is below), and the microtesla per LSB, which depends on the configured range — readings are raw LSB counts.

Magnetic field

Bars are scaled to the largest magnitude seen since connecting, so a small field still shows movement.

Registers

Register access is per 16-bit word and the register number goes on the wire as it is. Values are 16-bit; the NVRAM map starts around 0x10.
